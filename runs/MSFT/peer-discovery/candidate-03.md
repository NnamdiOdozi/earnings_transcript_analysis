Microsoft‘s stock delivered a modest 12-month return, but how does it truly stack up against peers thriving in the AI and cloud boom? As of January 21, 2026, a closer look reveals Microsoft‘s leading profitability, robust revenue growth, and a valuation comparable to mega-cap peers. Its dominant position in AI and cloud, with Azure maintaining a strong second place in market share and experiencing significant growth, continues to offer strong ongoing upside.

- MSFT’s 46.3% operating margin leads peers, reflecting strong profitability from diversified software, cloud, and enterprise services.
- MSFT’s 15.6% revenue growth, outpacing peers, is driven by strong Azure cloud adoption and enterprise software demand, fueled by AI.
- MSFT stock gained 4.4% with a 31.5 PE, reflecting growth expectations, though AI momentum shifts occasionally favored GOOGL and AAPL.
