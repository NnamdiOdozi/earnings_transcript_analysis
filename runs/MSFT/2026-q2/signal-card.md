# Signal Card: MSFT — 2026-q2

## Reported Financial Performance
- **reported** (AMY HOOD): Revenue was $81.3 billion, up 17% year-over-year.
  > "This quarter, revenue was $81.3 billion, up 17% and 15% in constant currency." — seg-0004
- **reported** (AMY HOOD): Earnings per share was $4.14.
  > "Earnings per share was $4.14, an increase of 24 and 21% in constant currency, when adjusted for the impact from our investment in OpenAI." — seg-0004
- **reported** (AMY HOOD): Microsoft Cloud revenue was $51.5 billion, up 26% year-over-year.
  > "Microsoft Cloud revenue was $51.5 billion and grew 26% and 24% in constant currency." — seg-0004

## Costs Margins Efficiency
- **reported** (AMY HOOD): Operating margin was 47%, up year-over-year.
  > "Operating margins increased year-over-year to 47%, ahead of expectations." — seg-0004

## Cash Flow Capital Allocation
- **reported** (AMY HOOD): Capital expenditures were $37.5 billion.
  > "Capital expenditures were $37.5 billion, and this quarter, roughly two thirds of our capex was on short-lived assets, primarily GPUs and CPUs." — seg-0004

## Operational Performance
- **reported** (SATYA NADELLA): Microsoft 365 Copilot paid seats reached 15 million.
  > "We saw accelerating seat growth quarter-over-quarter and now have 15 million paid Microsoft 365 Copilot seats, and multiples more enterprise Chat users." — seg-0003

## Current Guidance
- **forward_looking** (AMY HOOD): Q3 revenue guidance is $80.65 billion to $81.75 billion, representing 15% to 17% growth.
  > "We expect revenue of $80.65 to $81.75 billion or growth of 15% to %17 with continued strong growth across our commercial businesses, partially offset by our consumer businesses." — seg-0004
- **forward_looking** (AMY HOOD): Azure Q3 revenue growth guidance is 37% to 38% in constant currency.
  > "In Azure, we expect Q3 revenue growth to be between 37% and 38% in constant currency against a prior year comparable that included significantly accelerating growth rates in both Q3 and Q4." — seg-0004

## Capacity Supply Execution
- **reported** (AMY HOOD): Management stated customer demand continues to exceed available supply, an ongoing capacity constraint.
  > "Our customer demand continues to exceed our supply." — seg-0004

## Qa Insight
- **reported** (Keith Weiss, Morgan Stanley): An analyst raised concern that capex growth is outpacing expected Azure growth, questioning ROI on capex spend.
  > "CapEx is growing faster than we expected, and maybe Azure is growing a little bit slower than we expected." — seg-0005
- **reported** (AMY HOOD): CFO stated that if newly delivered GPU capacity had been allocated solely to Azure, Azure growth would have exceeded 40%, illustrating capacity is being split across multiple priorities, not just Azure.
  > "if I had taken the GPUs that just came online in Q1 and Q2, in terms of GPUs, and allocated them all to Azure, the KPI would have been over 40." — seg-0006

## Risk
- **forward_looking** (AMY HOOD): Azure Q3 guidance of 37% to 38% growth appears capacity-constrained rather than demand-constrained, since management states demand continues to exceed supply even as guidance implies deceleration from this quarter's growth.
  > "Our customer demand continues to exceed our supply." — seg-0004

## Demand Activity
- **reported** (AMY HOOD): Commercial remaining performance obligation (RPO) increased to $625 billion, up 110% year-over-year.
  > "Commercial remaining performance obligation, which continues to be reported net of reserves, increased to $625 billion and was up 110% year-over-year with a weighted average duration of approximately two and a half years." — seg-0004
- **reported** (Amy Hood): Commercial bookings increased 230% YoY (228% in constant currency), driven by the large Azure commitment from OpenAI and the Anthropic commitment, plus healthy core annuity sales growth.
  > "Commercial bookings increased 230% and 228% in constant currency driven by the previously announced large Azure commitment from OpenAI that reflects multi-year demand needs, as well as the previously announced Anthropic commitment from November, and healthy growth across our core annuity sales motions." — seg-0004
- **reported** (Amy Hood): Approximately 45% of commercial RPO is attributable to OpenAI, meaning a large share of the forward demand backlog is concentrated in a single customer relationship.
  > "Approximately 45% of our commercial RPO balance is from OpenAI." — seg-0004
