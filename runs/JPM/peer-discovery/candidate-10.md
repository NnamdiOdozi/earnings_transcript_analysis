# One of Wall Street’s Highest-Quality Stocks Is Becoming More Interesting 

This market leader isn’t exactly cheap, but its valuation looks more reasonable than it has in a while.

 Austin Taggart, CFA Jun 18, 2026

JPMorgan Chase & Co

JPMorgan Chase has long been one of the highest-quality financial stocks to own, thanks to the company’s market leadership across business lines, wide economic moat, and adept capital allocation under CEO Jamie Dimon. A premium price tag has likely left valuation-focused investors on the sidelines. But now that it is trading closer to Morningstar’s $311 fair value estimate, the stock is becoming more compelling. Morningstar Chief US Market Strategist Dave Sekera explained on a recent episode of The Morning Filter podcast why JPMorgan remains one of the best core stocks to own—and why long-term investors should be ready to buy on a pullback.

We view JPMorgan Chase’s strategy as an industry outlier in the best possible way. The company has meticulously constructed a comprehensive ecosystem that continues to distance itself from competitors, boasting the top market share in global investment banking fees, institutional trading revenue, domestic deposit market share, payment volume, domestic merchant acquiring, and credit card issuance. While each segment is a formidable competitor on its own, we believe the whole is greater than the sum of its parts, allowing the company to forge deeper consumer and commercial relationships more profitably than competing institutions can.

## Economic Moat Rating

We believe that JPMorgan has a defensible intangible asset in the reputation and capability of its investment banking arm, durable cost advantages across its consumer and commercial banks, and switching costs that span all three operating segments. Returns on tangible common equity have averaged 17.1% over the past decade, materially outearning the 9.5% cost of equity, despite operating in a challenging banking environment that featured historically low interest rates and long stretches of flat to inverted yield curves. We expect returns on tangible common equity to improve to 20.3% in the coming decade as the bank expands its branch presence to improve its market share of low-cost deposits and increases its customer product density.

## Fair Value Estimate for JPMorgan Stock

Our $311 fair value estimate equates to 2.8 times our 2026 projected tangible book value. In our long-term forecast, fee income growth rises to 6.0%, as we now see a stronger runway for revenue generation. We forecast net new asset flows to compress from 6.5% in the trailing decade to 5.6% in the coming decade. Over the decade to come, we see durable revenue growth on an asset base that we forecast to compound at 10.4% annually. We anticipate that investment banking revenue should compound at a 3.0% annual rate. We expect roughly 3.7% average annual trading revenue growth, implying a gradual normalization from recently elevated growth rates. We forecast net interest income growth of 8.7% in 2026, 3.4% in 2027, and 2.8% in 2028, as the headwinds of rate cuts should be more than offset by earning asset growth.

## Risk and Uncertainty

JPMorgan is sensitive to largely external variables like economic growth, changes in interest rates, and asset price levels. Substantial cyclicality in capital markets adds uncertainty to our forecasts for institutional trading and investment banking. JPMorgan must navigate complex and sometimes conflicting regulations across its 66-country footprint. Strict compliance with anti-money-laundering and know-your-customer regulations is critical. The company must continually lure and retain top-tier talent, as this is essential to maintaining a marquee banking franchise amid intense competition in global financial services.

## JPMorgan Bulls Say

- JPMorgan Chase could white-label its infrastructure to small and midsize banks, generating further revenue diversification with high margins.
- A structural macroeconomic shift may lead to a prolonged, steeper yield curve, providing a tailwind to net interest income.
- Increased incorporation of agentic artificial intelligence in the trading operation may enable significant increases in executing trading volume with a similar headcount.

## JPMorgan Bears Say

- CEO Dimon’s eventual succession remains one of the largest key-person risks in the financial sector.
- As the G20-backed Project Agorá for wholesale cross-border payments scales, the correspondent banking system could become commoditized and threaten high-margin payment volume.
- The proliferation of financial technology platforms that pay higher yields on deposits could raise funding costs for all banks.
